---
name: Study flow compatibility
description: Product decisions behind Phase 2 eligibility, Recovery scheduling, and post-course Maths revision.
---

## Phase 2 prerequisites
Preserve the established positive-total, completed Maths course and NCERT goals. Do not invent additional completion thresholds for the ongoing daily workout, practice, and vocabulary routines.

**Why:** The final specification required checking the existing phase system, not replacing its prerequisites or removing already-working access.

**How to apply:** Distinguish course completion from daily participation when changing phase eligibility, and keep each selected batch's course progress independent.

## Recovery scheduling
Sunday has a dedicated Recovery flow, but an accepted Recovery Quest can be completed through actual review on any weekday.

**Why:** Recovery already worked on any day. A Sunday-only eligibility gate would remove working functionality; the specification requested a Sunday flow, not that new restriction.

**How to apply:** Keep the scheduled Sunday recommendation separate from reward eligibility. Ordinary review must not create a Recovery entitlement.

## Post-course Maths revision
Actual Maths-card review from the selected, fully completed Maths course can satisfy its existing daily Maths goal once. It must not reopen completed lectures or grant their lecture XP again.

**Why:** The specification requires continuing Maths revision after Phase 2 unlock, while preserving completed course history and existing reward amounts.

**How to apply:** Use the existing daily reward, preserve card and batch identity, and route the Maths daily recommendation to revision even when another subject has a next lecture.
